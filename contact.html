YOU DO NOT NEED TO HAVE IT ALL ORGANIZED

Start with the messy version of the question.

If you knew exactly why cash was tight, which trips were profitable, or what your best growth move was, you probably would not need a CFO conversation. Bring what you know. We can work from there.

01

“Sales are up, but cash still feels tight.”

That can point to seasonality, inventory, collections, deposits, debt service, owner draws, or margin—not necessarily a sales problem.

02

“Our trips sell, but I don't know what we actually make.”

We can separate revenue from real trip contribution after fees, comp spots, staff travel, cancellations, and variable costs.

03

“We're growing, but I'm not sure what to grow next.”

Compare the cash requirements, margin, capacity, repeatability, and risk of the options before committing resources.
