04 / TRIP ECONOMICS

Dive Trip Profitability & Planning

A trip can sell out and still disappoint financially. Know the economics before you commit the cash.

Build the trip model around the costs that actually matter:

  • True break-even headcount
  • Airfare, lodging, liveaboard, and transfer costs
  • Group-leader and free-spot economics
  • Card and transaction fees
  • Currency/FX exposure
  • Staff travel and labor costs
  • Cancellation risk and reserves
  • Target profit per traveler and per trip
Best for: shops, resorts, operators, and travel businesses that want to price trips intentionally instead of backing into a margin after everything is sold.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we work together

Do I need a CFO if I already have a bookkeeper or CPA?

Possibly. Bookkeepers and CPAs play important roles. Fractional Controller/CFO work focuses on turning the financial information into operating decisions, forecasting, margin strategy, cash planning, and management accountability.

Do you only work with scuba businesses?

Dive businesses are the core niche, but the same model can fit adjacent outdoor, adventure, travel, marine, hospitality, and mission-driven organizations where seasonality, inventory, trips, or complex service lines create similar financial challenges.

What if my books are messy?

That is not unusual. The first step is determining whether the numbers are decision-ready. If cleanup or stronger accounting processes are needed, the Controller level of support can address that before deeper CFO analysis.

Can we start with one project?

Yes. The Dive Profit Blueprint or a focused trip-profitability project can be a practical way to start before deciding whether ongoing support makes sense.

What happens after I contact you?

We identify what you are trying to solve, what information is available, and the smallest useful scope of work. If ongoing support is not necessary, the recommendation should reflect that.
