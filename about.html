WHY THE NICHE MATTERS

A dive business is not a generic small business.

Your economics can span training, retail, rentals, service, air fills, travel, lodging, charters, seasonal staffing, deposits, inventory, and international payments—sometimes all at once.

That matters because financial advice gets much more useful when the person giving it understands how the business actually works.

“The goal is not to turn a dive business into a spreadsheet. It is to use the numbers to protect what owners are building—and make the business stronger.”
Profitability by business lineSeparate what looks busy from what actually contributes profit.
Seasonal cash planningPrepare for the months when deposits, inventory, payroll, and trip payments do not line up neatly.
Trip economicsModel real break-even, free spots, fees, staff travel, FX, and target margin.
Inventory and working capitalSee when gear on the shelf is helping sales—or quietly trapping cash.
Owner decisionsKnow what can safely be distributed, reinvested, hired, financed, or expanded.
